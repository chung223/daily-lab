# 方向感是動詞 · 哲學指南針

**🧭 Compass × Philosophy · 方向感是動詞**

A philosophical compass that guides you through life's directions with wisdom from philosophy cards. Click to rotate and discover profound quotes and insights.

![方向感是動詞](https://img.shields.io/badge/Daily%20Lab-2026--08--15-FF6B6B?style=for-the-badge)

## Features

- **Interactive Compass** — Click anywhere to rotate and select your direction
- **Philosophy Cards** — Each direction reveals a philosophical insight
- **Auto-Rotate Animation** — Compass automatically rotates on load
- **Confetti Celebration** — Visual feedback on interactions
- **Memphis Design Style** — Colorful decorative shapes and vibrant aesthetics
- **Chinese Wisdom** — Classical philosophy quotes in Traditional Chinese

## Design Elements

- Memphis decorative shapes (circles, triangles, zigzags)
- Vibrant color palette: coral, teal, yellow, pink, purple
- Multiple font families: Anybody, Noto Serif TC, Caveat
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Touch-friendly for mobile devices
