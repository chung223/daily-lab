# 🪪 IFYE 2026 世界友誼護照 | World Passport

**IFYE 2026 World Passport** — A digital passport celebrating international friendship and the International Four-H Exchange program. Features 9 delegates from 5 nations with 51 host stations across the globe.

- **Delegate Registry** — Interactive cards showcasing 9 representatives from Philippines, Korea, Thailand, Switzerland, Ghana, and Austria
