# 看不見的線·連結觀測所 | Invisible Threads Observatory

An interactive visualization exploring the invisible connections between people, thoughts, and emotions.

![Preview](preview.gif)

## About

This project visualizes the concept of "invisible threads" that connect us — those moments when you think of someone and they happen to reach out, or the synchronized experiences that seem too coincidental to be random.

## Features

- **Interactive Canvas**: Drag to explore the constellation of connected ideas
- **Node-based Visualization**: Six thematic nodes representing different aspects of human connection
- **Dynamic Thread Connections**: Threads that animate between related concepts
- **Click Interaction**: Click on nodes or cards to learn more about each concept
- **Responsive Design**: Works on both desktop and mobile

## Themes Explored

- 🧠 Quantum Entanglement of Friendship
- 💧 Dissolving Boundaries (Flow State)
- 📐 Emotional Vector Mathematics
- 🎮 The Value of Play
- 🌊 Currents of Fate
- ✨ Everyday Magic
